Dr. Manobendro Majumder, DVM

Upazila Livestock Officer, Baliakandi, Rajbari
Department of Livestock Services, Government of Bangladesh

I grew up in rural Bangladesh, where I saw first-hand how much a family's wellbeing depends on the health of its cattle, goats and poultry. That experience inspired me to become a veterinarian — and later, to dedicate my career to public veterinary service.

As Upazila Livestock Officer, I lead the government's livestock services in Baliakandi upazila: running the upazila veterinary hospital, supervising field staff, coordinating vaccination and breed-improvement programs, and advising hundreds of farm families every month.

Education & Qualifications

Professional Experience

  • Upazila Livestock Officer, Baliakandi, Rajbari — Department of Livestock Services (2021 – present, sample). Leading veterinary and livestock extension services for the entire upazila.
  • Veterinary Surgeon, Upazila Livestock Office — Department of Livestock Services (2016 – 2021, sample). Clinical treatment, surgery and artificial insemination services.
  • Veterinary Field Practitioner — Private and NGO-supported practice (2012 – 2016, sample). On-farm treatment and farmer advisory in rural communities.

Key Achievements

  • Coordinated upazila-wide FMD and PPR vaccination campaigns reaching tens of thousands of animals each year.
  • Expanded artificial insemination coverage, improving dairy productivity for smallholder farmers.
  • Trained hundreds of farmers and youth entrepreneurs in modern dairy, beef fattening and poultry management.
  • Strengthened disease surveillance and rapid-response reporting during avian influenza and lumpy skin disease alerts.

Memberships

  • Registered Veterinary Practitioner, Bangladesh Veterinary Council (BVC)
  • Member, The Bangladesh Veterinary Association (BVA)
