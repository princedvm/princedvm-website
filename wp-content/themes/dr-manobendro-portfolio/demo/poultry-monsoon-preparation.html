The monsoon brings welcome rain to our fields — and a wave of poultry health problems: coccidiosis, fowl cholera, Newcastle disease and fungal feed contamination all spike in the wet months. A little preparation now saves a lot of loss later.

A pre-monsoon checklist

  1. Repair the shed roof and raise the floor. A dry litter bed is your first line of defence against coccidiosis.
  2. Complete vaccinations before the rains. Make sure Newcastle disease and fowl cholera boosters are up to date.
  3. Store feed off the ground in sealed containers — damp feed grows toxic moulds within days.
  4. Improve ventilation while keeping rain out; humid, stagnant air spreads respiratory disease.
  5. Provide clean drinking water. Flood water carries pathogens — never let birds drink from puddles.

The Upazila Livestock Office runs free Newcastle disease vaccination drives for backyard poultry before every monsoon. Watch for announcements at your union parishad office, or contact us directly to register your village.
