Many farmers in Baliakandi still rely on natural service with local bulls. Artificial insemination (AI) with quality-certified semen is safer, cheaper in the long run, and the fastest path to a more productive herd.

The benefits in plain terms

  • Better calves: crossbred heifers from proven sires can produce several times more milk than local cattle.
  • Disease control: AI eliminates the breeding-related spread of brucellosis and other infections.
  • No bull-keeping costs: feeding and housing a breeding bull is expensive and often dangerous.
  • Record keeping: every AI service is documented, which helps plan calving, drying-off and vaccination.

When to call the AI technician

Timing is everything. Watch for standing heat — the cow stands still when mounted by others. The best window for insemination is roughly 12 hours after the first signs of standing heat. Call the office as soon as you notice heat signs in the morning; we aim to provide same-day service.
