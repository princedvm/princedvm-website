About Me

Serving the farmers and animals of Baliakandi

I am a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine currently serving as the Upazila Livestock Officer of Baliakandi, Rajbari, under the Department of Livestock Services, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh. My work combines clinical veterinary practice with public-sector livestock development — from treating sick cattle at the upazila veterinary hospital to leading vaccination campaigns, breed improvement programs and farmer training across the upazila.

I believe healthy animals build healthy livelihoods. Every day, my team and I work to bring modern, science-based animal healthcare to the doorsteps of smallholder farmers.

What I Do

Veterinary & Extension Services

Livestock Treatment

Clinical diagnosis and treatment of cattle, buffalo, goats and sheep at the upazila veterinary hospital and through on-farm visits.

Poultry Health

Disease prevention, treatment and biosecurity guidance for commercial and backyard poultry farms across Baliakandi.

Vaccination Programs

Planning and delivery of government vaccination campaigns against FMD, anthrax, PPR, Newcastle disease and more.

Artificial Insemination

Breed improvement through artificial insemination services and reproductive health management for dairy cattle.

Farmer Training

Hands-on training for smallholder farmers and youth entrepreneurs on dairy management, fattening, fodder and biosecurity.

Disease Surveillance

Monitoring, reporting and rapid response for livestock and zoonotic disease outbreaks in coordination with district authorities.
