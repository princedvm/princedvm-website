The Upazila Livestock Office, Baliakandi provides the following veterinary and livestock development services. Most government services are free of charge or offered at a nominal government-fixed fee.

Clinical Treatment of Livestock

Diagnosis and treatment of diseases of cattle, buffalo, goats and sheep — including digestive, respiratory, reproductive and parasitic conditions — at the veterinary hospital and through farm visits.

Poultry Health & Biosecurity

Vaccination scheduling, disease prevention and biosecurity audits for commercial layer, broiler and backyard poultry, plus duck and pigeon health services.

Vaccination Campaigns

Mass vaccination programs against foot-and-mouth disease (FMD), anthrax, black quarter, PPR, rabies, Newcastle disease, fowl cholera and duck plague.

Artificial Insemination & Breed Improvement

AI services using quality-certified semen, pregnancy diagnosis, infertility management and guidance on improved cattle and goat breeds.

Farmer Training & Advisory

Training sessions and one-to-one advisory on dairy management, beef fattening, goat rearing, fodder cultivation, feed formulation and farm record keeping.

Disease Surveillance & Reporting

Continuous monitoring of livestock and zoonotic diseases, outbreak investigation, sample collection for laboratory diagnosis and coordination with district and national authorities.
